# Heel & Serpent embroidery files

Built by `merch/embroidery.py` (Oct 15, 2026) for the soft drape tee and French terry hood. Rebuild with
`python merch/embroidery.py` from heel-and-serpent/.

Every file is a transparent PNG at 300 DPI, at its real stitched size, in flat thread colours only.
Built a little above Printful's embroidery limits: lettering at least 0.27 in tall (limit 0.25), lines at
least 0.055 in (limit 0.05), gaps at least 0.035 in, no more than 3 thread colours per file (limit 6).
Each `*_check.png` shows the file with any line under the limit in red and any tight gap in blue. What
red remains is serif tips and sharp corners, which an embroiderer shrinks to small stubs anyway.

**Thread colours.** The hex codes are targets. Pick the closest thread the shop offers. The tonal mark on
iron needs a dark charcoal thread, a shade lighter than the garment; if the shop's standard palette has no
close match, use its custom-colour option. Ember should read as a burnt orange, not a bright safety orange.

**What changed from the screen marks** (thread can't hold the fine detail): the Monogram loses its rule and
the serpent's forked tongue, its tail is thickened and its eye enlarged; the Seal goes to 4.5 in with single
rings, bolder ring lettering in Garamond ExtraBold and no serpent eye; the nape and sleeve lines are set in
Inter Black (the brand's label face), because Garamond's hairlines can't hold at a quarter inch.

**For samples** (personal use only; nothing is sold until the trademark review): upload the file for each
placement and ask for a stitch-out photo or a single sample before any run. On soft fabric, ask for lighter
stitch density and a soft mesh backing.
